# Owner Dashboard Service — Offer One-Pager

*Drafted 2026-07-12. Track 2 — designed as the UPSELL inside the Direct Booking Audit, not a standalone cold offer.*

---

## The offer

**"Your data, finally readable."** Client sends raw PMS/POS/booking-engine exports; gets back clean data and a monthly owner dashboard — revenue, channel mix, OTA vs. direct share, top items/room types — plus a one-page reading guide.

**The pitch, in one line:**
> "You have the data. You just can't see it. I'll turn your PMS/POS exports into a one-page monthly view of where money comes from and where it leaks."

## Pricing

| Item | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Dashboard setup | **5–10M IDR** | Depends on source count/mess level. Fixed after scoping the exports. |
| Monthly refresh + maintenance | **1–2M IDR/mo** | Data in → updated dashboard out. No analysis calls included. |
| Ad-hoc analysis memo | **1.5M IDR** | Written answer to one business question. Optional. |

## Who it's for / not for

- **For:** villa/hotel owners already through the Direct Booking Audit (warm, trust established), and restaurants with POS systems and no analyst.
- **Not for:** cold prospects — nobody wakes up wanting a dashboard, so this is never the first conversation. Not for businesses that want a live analyst on call.

## Why this stays second (from the 2026-07-12 interview)

- The build is the work Oka loves most (messy data cleanup). The *sell* requires patient explaining to non-technical owners — the thing he hates. Solution: sell it only to warm clients where the audit already proved the value, or bundle it as a line item.
- Standalone economics are slow: 5–10M setups + 1–2M/mo needs many clients to matter. As an upsell it's near-pure margin on existing relationships.

## Boundaries (non-negotiable)

- Deliverable is the dashboard + written guide. Explaining happens on paper, not in meetings.
- Maintenance retainer covers refreshes, not consulting. Questions become paid memos.
- Scope-capped: agreed sources only; new source = new scoping.

Offer one restaurant friend a **free POS-to-dashboard build** in exchange for: (a) a testimonial, (b) the price they'd honestly have paid, (c) referral to one other owner. That gives a portfolio piece for both this track and the remote gigs (Track 3 uses the same skill).

## The offer

A fixed-price, written **Direct Booking Audit** for Bali villas and small hotels: a full review of how a property wins (or loses) direct bookings, delivered as a report the owner can act on — with or without me.

**The pitch, in one line:**
> "You pay OTAs 15–20% on every booking. I'll show you exactly why guests aren't booking direct, and what it costs you per month — fixed price, written report, 10 working days."

## What's in the audit

1. **Google Ads account review** — wasted spend, missing brand-protection campaigns, competitor bidding on their name.
2. **SEO / search visibility check** — does the property rank for its own name + "direct" queries; OTA vs. own-site SERP share.
3. **Booking funnel review** — website-to-booking path, mobile experience, rate parity vs. OTA, booking engine friction.
4. **The number** — estimated monthly commission leak in IDR, and the 3 highest-ROI fixes ranked.

**Deliverable:** written report (PDF) + one 30-minute handover call, maximum. No workshops, no ongoing hand-holding at this stage.

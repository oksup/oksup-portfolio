# Remote Productized Audits — Offer One-Pager

*Drafted 2026-07-12. Track 3 — Oka's chosen starting point. Overseas, async, USD.*

---

## The offer

Fixed-scope, fixed-price **written audits and data-cleanup gigs** for overseas small businesses, sold through freelance platforms (Upwork first). Zero calls by design — everything delivered as a document.

**Two productized gigs:**

1. **Google Ads Account Audit** — wasted spend, structure, tracking, competitor exposure; ranked fix list. Delivered as PDF in 5 working days.
2. **Messy Data Cleanup + Dashboard** — client sends exports (CSV/Excel/POS/CRM); gets back clean data + a working dashboard (Looker Studio/Sheets) + a one-page "how to read it."

## Pricing

| Gig | Launch price | Target price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Ads Audit | $150 | $300–500 | Launch price only until 5 reviews. |
| Data Cleanup + Dashboard | $200 | $400–800 | Scope-capped: max 3 data sources, 1 dashboard. |

**Target math:** at target prices, ~6–8 gigs/month ≈ 20M IDR. At launch prices this is a review-farming phase, not income.

## Who it's for / not for

- **For:** overseas small businesses (US/AU/EU) who treat $300–500 as cheap, want a deliverable not a consultant, and communicate async.
- **Not for:** anyone requiring standing calls, ongoing management, or "quick call to discuss?" clients — decline politely, point to the gig scope.

## Boundaries (non-negotiable)

- Async only. No scheduled calls; Loom video walkthrough max if a client insists.
- Fixed scope, fixed price. Scope creep = new gig.
- English written comms — templates for kickoff, delivery, and revision requests to keep writing time down.

## Honest risks (from the 2026-07-12 interview — eyes open)

- Cold start: zero reviews means weeks of low-ball pricing before traction.
- Platform race-to-the-bottom against $50 audit sellers — differentiation is the sample report quality.
- Slowest of the three tracks to first revenue.

## Week-one validation test

1. Build **one portfolio-grade sample audit** (anonymized — use a dummy or own account, never Nakula client data).
2. Publish Upwork profile + the Ads Audit gig at $150.
3. Send 10 proposals on matching job posts using the sample as proof.

**Success signal:** 1 paid gig or 3+ genuine client conversations within 2 weeks. Zero of either → fix the sample/positioning before grinding proposals.
